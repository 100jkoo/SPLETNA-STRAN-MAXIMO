A note from Trzin

I started in textiles at sixteen, as a student, and I have spent twenty-five years helping to build Maximo step by step. The company has been making fabric since 1991.

Our customers make workwear, uniforms and medical wear in Slovenia, Croatia, Austria, Italy, Serbia and Bosnia and Herzegovina. What they need is simple: the fabric they ordered, in the colour they ordered, on time, and the same again when they reorder.

That is why we keep the fabrics our customers use most on the shelf, and why everything else is made to order with a sample first.

If you need a fabric we do not have yet, write to us. That is usually how our new articles begin.
