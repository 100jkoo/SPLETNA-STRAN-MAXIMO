500D or 1000D?

D stands for denier, the thickness of the yarn. 500D CORDURA® is lighter and more flexible, the usual choice for garments, knee and seat reinforcements and light packs. 1000D is heavier, with more abrasion resistance and tensile strength, for tool bags, tactical gear and heavy-duty panels.

Bars show the relative difference between the two yarns, not test values. Technical data sheets with measured values are available for every article.

In the garment

Why workwear makers choose CORDURA®.

CORDURA® goes where a garment wears out first: knees, pockets, seat, cuffs and bags. These are the properties customers ask us about most.

Abrasion resistance

High-tenacity nylon 6.6 stands up to scraping, kneeling and dragging far longer than cotton or polycotton.

Tear strength

Strong yarns and ripstop grids keep a small cut from turning into a long tear.

Light for its strength

The same durability at a lower weight than heavy cotton canvas, so garments and bags stay comfortable.

Colour that lasts

Resists fading and shrinking and is usually machine washable, so garments look right for longer.

Coated and water repellent

PU coated versions for bags and equipment, water repellent finishes for outerwear panels.

Built for the job

Hi-vis, aramid, antistatic, stretch and military versions, so the toughness goes where the garment needs it.

Questions

CORDURA® fabrics, answered.

Short answers to what buyers and garment makers ask before ordering. For anything else, write to us.

What is CORDURA®?
CORDURA® is a brand of durable fabrics, mostly woven from high-tenacity nylon 6.6. It is known for abrasion and tear resistance and is used in workwear, military and tactical gear, outdoor equipment, bags and luggage.
What is the difference between 500D and 1000D CORDURA®?
D stands for denier, the yarn thickness. 500D is lighter and more flexible and suits garments and reinforcements. 1000D is heavier, with higher abrasion resistance and tensile strength, for bags, tactical gear and heavy-duty panels.
What is ripstop?
Ripstop has thicker reinforcing yarns woven in a grid about 5 to 8 mm apart. It keeps the fabric light and strong and stops small tears from spreading.
Do you keep CORDURA® fabrics on stock?
Yes, two qualities: CORDURA 200 (100% CORDURA®, 200 g/m², grey and black) and CDR BLEND 260 (50% cotton, 50% CORDURA®). All other articles are produced to order, custom colours from 300 m.
Can CORDURA® be combined with cotton, aramid or stretch fibres?
Yes. We produce blends with cotton for comfort, with meta- and para-aramid for protective workwear, with T400 for stretch, and with an antistatic yarn. Hi-vis yellow and orange are also available.
Do you produce CORDURA® for military specifications?
Yes. Our military articles are produced to the reference specifications listed in the table. Test reports are confirmed per order.
Can Maximo also sew the finished garments?
Yes. Besides producing the fabric, Maximo sews finished garments to your sample or specification, so fabric and garment come from one partner.
