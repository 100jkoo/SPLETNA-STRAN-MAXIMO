About us

We make the fabric. You make the workwear.

Maximo Tekstil develops, produces and stocks fabric for workwear, protective clothing and medical wear. Our customers are the companies that cut and sew it. We have done this from Trzin, near Ljubljana, since 1991.

What we do

One subject since 1991: fabric for clothes people work in.

Trousers that kneel on concrete all day. Coats washed at 90 °C every week. Jackets worn next to a welding arc. Each of them asks something different of the fabric, and choosing the right construction is most of the job.

That is what we work on every day. We know which blend keeps its colour through industrial laundry, which weave holds up at the knee, and which standard a protective garment has to pass. We keep the qualities you use most on stock, produce new ones to your specification and, when you need finished garments, we sew them too.

How we work

We start from your garment, not from our catalogue.

01Tell us about the garment

What it is, who wears it, how it is washed and which standard it has to meet. That decides the fabric.

02Talk about price openly

Tell us what you pay today. We adjust yarn, weight, blend and finish until the fabric and the price both work for you.

03Check a sample

A cut of any stock fabric within days. For a new article we make a sample to your specification, usually in one to two weeks.

04Order, and order again

Stock ships from Trzin the same day when ordered by 14:00. New articles are produced to the approved sample, re-orders to the same specification.

What you can hold us to

Four things we do not bend on.

On stock means on stock

What we list as on stock is lying in our warehouse in Trzin, ready to cut and ship.

Small orders are welcome

One roll from stock, or a cut from 1 m with a small surcharge. Your own colour from 300 m.

Numbers on the safe side

The values on our data sheets come from our own lab and are set conservatively. Independent labs usually rate our fabrics the same or higher.

A straight answer

If a fabric is wrong for your job, we tell you before you order, not after.
