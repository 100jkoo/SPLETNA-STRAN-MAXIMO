About us

Fabric for people who work. Since 1991.

Maximo Tekstil is a textile company from Trzin, Slovenia. For more than 30 years we have developed and supplied fabrics for workwear, protective clothing and medical wear. Today we also sew the finished garments.

Who we are

Made for washing, wearing and working.

We specialise in fabrics for protective clothing and workwear: cotton, polycotton, stretch, flame retardant cotton, modacrylic and aramid blends, hi-vis and CORDURA®. What our customers need most is a fabric that keeps its colour and its strength through hundreds of industrial washes, and that is where we put our attention.

Our customers are workwear manufacturers, uniform makers, laundries and brands. We keep the most used qualities on stock in our warehouse in Trzin, produce new articles to order, and develop custom samples to each customer's specification.

We are a licensed CORDURA® manufacturer and our fabrics are certified to OEKO-TEX® Standard 100.

Our mission

Reliable fabric for professional workwear.

We supply high quality fabrics for professional clothing, from industrial workwear to uniforms for police, firefighters, construction and healthcare. We make custom samples to each client's specification and work as a dependable, ethical manufacturer.

Our vision

Slovenian textile know-how, at home in Europe.

We want to be known across Europe for innovation, quality and care for our customers: anticipating what the next generation of workwear needs, and investing in people, technology and sustainable materials.
