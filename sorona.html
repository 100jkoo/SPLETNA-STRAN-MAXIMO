What is Sorona®

A polyester family fibre, partly grown from plants.

Sorona® is a PTT polymer (polytrimethylene terephthalate). One of its two building blocks, Bio-PDO, is made by fermenting plant sugar instead of refining oil. Spun into yarn and blended with cotton and polyester, it gives a fabric soft handle and lasting mechanical stretch.

Why it stretches and comes back

The Sorona® molecule has a kinked shape that behaves like a coil spring. Pull the fabric and the chain extends, release it and it returns. Many of the blends below reach comfortable stretch without any elastane, which helps them hold up to industrial washing and keep their shape garment after garment.

In the garment

What the wearer and the laundry notice.

Sorona® changes how a workwear fabric behaves during a shift and after many washes. These are the properties customers ask us about most.

Stretch and recovery

Moves with kneeling, climbing and reaching, then returns to shape. Less bagging at knees and elbows.

Soft handle

A smoother, softer feel than a standard polycotton of the same weight, welcome in shirts and customer-facing uniforms.

Shape retention

Garments keep their fit through repeated wash cycles, so a uniform still looks like a uniform at the end of its life.

Colour that lasts

Dyes evenly and holds its shade well, which matters for corporate colours that have to match across reorders.

Partly plant-based

37% of the Sorona® polymer comes from renewable plant sources, lowering the share of oil-based fibre in the blend.

Built for the job

Reinforced, weatherproof, thermal and flame retardant versions, so the stretch goes into the garment that needs it.

Questions
Sorona® fabrics, answered.
Short answers to what buyers and garment makers ask before ordering. For anything else, write to us.

    

      
What is Sorona®?
Sorona® is a PTT (polytrimethylene terephthalate) polymer fibre. 37% of the polymer is made from renewably sourced plant ingredients. Its kinked molecular shape gives fabrics soft handle and lasting stretch and recovery.
Is Sorona® a recycled fibre?
No. Sorona® is partly plant-based, not recycled. If you need recycled content, we also produce fabrics with recycled polyester such as REPREVE®.
Do Sorona® blends need elastane to stretch?
Not necessarily. The stretch comes from the Sorona® fibre itself, so many of our blends reach comfortable stretch without an elastane component. For higher stretch we adjust the Sorona® share, up to 20%.
Which Sorona® fabric is best for work trousers?
TerraFlex (220 g/m², 10% Sorona®) is our standard choice for work trousers in light industry. For tactical or fieldwork trousers, Vanguard (240 g/m²) adds a reinforcing grid.
Can Sorona® be used in flame retardant or weatherproof workwear?
Yes. EmberGuard is a flame retardant version for safety and welding garments, and Horizon and SummitShield are weatherproof fabrics for protective outerwear. Test reports and standards are confirmed per order.
Do you keep Sorona® fabrics on stock?
No. Sorona® articles are produced to order to your weight, construction and colour. Custom colours start from 300 m.
Can Maximo also sew the finished garments?
Yes. Besides producing the fabric, Maximo sews finished garments to your sample or specification, so fabric and garment come from one partner.
