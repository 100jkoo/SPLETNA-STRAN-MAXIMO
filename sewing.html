How it works

Three steps. One contact.

  1. Send a photo

    Of the garment you wear today, a sketch or a catalogue picture. Tell us how many pieces you need.

  2. Get a price and a sample

    We suggest the fabric, send a price per piece, then sew a sample for you to check fit and finish.

  3. We sew and deliver

    After your approval we produce the order, check it and deliver it to you.
